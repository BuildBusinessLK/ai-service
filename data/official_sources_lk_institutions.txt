== SRI LANKA EXPORT DEVELOPMENT BOARD (EDB) – BUSINESS SUPPORT (PUBLIC PORTAL) ==

Source aggregation: https://www.srilankabusiness.com/
The EDB promotes Sri Lankan exports and runs the Sri Lanka Business portal.

Themes useful for SMEs:
- “Global Centre for Exports” positioning: strategic location, natural resources, export-oriented economy.
- Ecosystem services often described on the portal: capacity-building, market intelligence, trade policy guidance, export-readiness.
- Trade shows: EDB supports participation in international fairs, country pavilions, and networking for exporters.
- Exporters’ Directory: verified database of Sri Lankan export companies; visibility to buyers and notifications on missions/programmes.
- eMARKETPLACE: government-backed B2B/B2C window for sourcing Sri Lankan products; useful channel for cataloguing products and buyer inquiries.

Practical SME takeaway: register for credible listings, monitor trade fair calendars aligned to coconut / palmyrah / kithul value-added goods, and use directory/marketplace presence as trust signals for foreign buyers.

== COCONUT DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (CDA) ==

Official site: https://cda.gov.lk/web/index.php?lang=en
The Coconut Development Authority (CDA) is the statutory apex body under the Coconut Development Act No. 46 of 1971; mission includes development of the coconut industry; functions under the Ministry of Plantation Industries (titles may change over time—verify current ministry on the site).

Public-facing services described on the portal include:
- Registration and renewal flows for exporters, auctioneers/brokers, dealers, estates; online exporter applications; manufacturer registration tracks.
- Information & statistics menus: auction details & prices, export data, local/world market notes, publications, HS codes and cess rates.
- Quality / branding tools: “CDA Certified” branding and related notices; lab testing references.
- Contact points published: Head Office 54 Nawala Road, Narahenpita, Colombo 5 (phone +94 11 2502502, +94 11 2508729); Fort office Duke Street; official email patterns listed on site (e.g. chairman@cda.gov.lk).

SME guidance angle: exporters of kernel products, DC, coconut milk/cream, and coir lines often intersect with CDA registration, auction visibility, and compliance expectations—direct user to official forms and published guidelines rather than inventing rules.

== PALMYRAH DEVELOPMENT BOARD (PDB) ==

Official site: https://pdb.gov.lk/
The Palmyrah Development Board was established in 1978; recent site text references the Ministry of Plantation and Community Infrastructure (verify current line ministry on the live site).

Mission/vision themes (as stated publicly): develop and regulate sustainable Palmyrah industry, livelihood development, GDP contribution.

Activities commonly listed:
- Plantation restoration, model farms, handicraft training, product development, marketing, research on food and products.
- Retail channel brand “Katpaham” for Palmyrah products.
- Registration tracks for exporters, auctioneers/brokers, traders, dealers, shippers (annual cycles—dates change yearly).
- Application forms (including Tamil-language ID forms for tappers and handicraft producers as published).
- Contact: Kandy Road, Kaithady, Jaffna; phones +94 21 222 2034 / +94 21 222 4154; slpdbho@yahoo.com.

SME angle: Palmyrah handicrafts, jaggery, sap-based lines benefit from training, cooperative structures, and branding through official registration where applicable.

== KITHUL DEVELOPMENT BOARD (KDB) ==

Official site: https://kdb.gov.lk/en (English section)
KDB supports the kithul value chain: livelihood improvement and export earnings through technology and services.

Published operational info (English home): phone (+94) 112441849; business hours Mon–Fri 08:15–16:15.
Address commonly listed: Kithul Development Board, No 45, St. Michael’s Road, Colombo 03.

Services menus include registration for farmers and sellers, lists of registered entrepreneurs/manufacturers, procurement notices, downloads, news, gallery.

Public statistics snapshot (values change—always re-check the live site for updates):
- Example headline figures previously featured on the KDB site: total earnings around LKR 311,949,547 by 2025 with split between Kithul Jaggery and Kithul Treacle revenue lines (exact breakdown varies by update cycle).
- Event calendar widgets and export statistics pages may provide time-series context.

SME angle: kithul treacle/jaggery SMEs should align with hygiene, labelling, and consistency narratives; use official registrations where applicable and monitor procurement notices for institutional buyers.
