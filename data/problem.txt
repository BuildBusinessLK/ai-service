 1. Key Problems in Coconut / Kithul / Palmyrah Industries (Sri Lanka)
 A. Farming & Production Problems
Low productivity of old palm trees (low yield per tree)
Climate change (drought, irregular rainfall)
Pest & disease issues (especially coconut mites, fungal diseases)
Lack of modern cultivation knowledge among small farmers
Poor replanting / aging plantations
Limited access to quality seedlings

 Impact: unstable raw material supply

B. Price & Market Problems
Highly volatile farm-gate prices
Middlemen controlling pricing
Farmers not knowing real market/export prices
No real-time price transparency

 Impact: farmers sell cheap, exporters gain more margin

 C. Processing & Value Addition Problems
Lack of modern machinery (small-scale producers)
Poor standardization of products (jaggery, oil, treacle)
Inconsistent quality between batches
Low shelf-life due to packaging issues
 D. Supply Chain Problems
Weak logistics from rural areas
High transport cost
Delays in collection and processing
No digital tracking of supply chain
 E. Certification & Export Problems
Complicated certification process (HACCP, GMP, ISO)
Lack of knowledge about export documentation
Difficulty accessing buyers
Poor branding of SME products

Related institutions:

Coconut Development Authority (CDA)
Export Development Board (EDB)
Kithul Development Board
Palmyrah Development Board
 F. Finance Problems
Lack of SME credit access
High collateral requirements
Seasonal cash flow instability
No credit scoring system for farmers
 G. Data & Knowledge Problems (VERY IMPORTANT for your AI agent)
Data is scattered across government PDFs and websites
No unified dashboard for:
prices
yield
export demand
certification rules
Farmers rely on word-of-mouth

 THIS is where your AI agent becomes powerful
